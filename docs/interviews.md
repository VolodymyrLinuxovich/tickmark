# Interviews: what auditors actually ask

Short notes from talks with people who have worked in accounting, audit, or a finance team. No full names.

The questions used for "Ask your books" come from the "Exact questions" lines below.

## Person 1: (role, e.g. Big 4 audit intern)

1. What did the auditor's request list look like, and which requests took longest to answer?
2. What questions did the auditor ask after the first answers?
3. Where did the evidence live, and how did you send it?
4. Would you trust a tool that gathers it automatically? What would make you not trust it?
